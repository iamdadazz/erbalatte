# Erbalatte — Product

## What it is
Erbalatte is the milk of Azienda Agricola La Corte (Fratelli Bergese, Monasterolo di Savigliano, Cuneo, Piemonte), a family farm now on its sixth generation. It is the first milk certified from **Agricoltura Simbiotica** (symbiotic agriculture), adopted in 2016 with the University of Turin and consortium ECOSI'. Mycorrhizal fungi improve soil life; polyphyte meadows bring back biodiversity (and the bees); the Friesian cows eat grass, hay and farm-grown cereals, with no chemical fertilisers, pesticides, GMOs or maize silage on the meadows. Manure goes to a biogas plant, which produces renewable electricity and natural fertiliser. Each cow wears a sensor that tracks steps, rumination and rest.

Proof (University of Turin & Pisa analyses): **+19% protein, +27% vitamin A, +25% vitamin E**. Described as sweet, delicate, fragrant and digestible.
